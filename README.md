# Lie Groups and Algebraic Groups

A. L. Onishchik, E. B. Vinberg. *Lie Groups and Algebraic Groups*.
Translated from the Russian by D. A. Leites. Springer Series in Soviet
Mathematics, Springer-Verlag, 1990 (Russian edition: Nauka, 1988).

The book introduces Lie groups, algebraic varieties and linear algebraic
groups through a sequence of problems with hints, and goes on to the
structure and classification of complex and real semisimple Lie groups and
the Levi decomposition; its Reference Chapter collects the tables of root
systems, representations, automorphisms and real forms.

This is an unofficial editorial edition, typeset anew in Typst. It keeps the
text, the problems and hints, the numbering, the tables and the subject
index of the printed book, and corrects the errors that could be
established: misprints, wrong references and citations, wrong table entries
and statements that are false as printed. Every correction is listed with
the printed reading and the reason in a separate PDF, and recorded in
[`corrections.json`](corrections.json) with how it was verified. Six
editorial notes, marked "\*1", "\*2", … and signed "— Ed.", explain the
corrections a reader would otherwise not understand; an edition without
them can be built from the sources. Computations of the book and a few of
its steps are checked with Sage and Lean ([`checks/`](checks/README.md)).
